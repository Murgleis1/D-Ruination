# Dreamstone Ruination TM set — draft v2 (2026-11-27, for the user's review)

User 2026-11-27: our own TM set to make it interesting; **90 TMs** ("meet in the middle"); classics at their Gen 3 numbers
(**TM06 Toxic, TM13 Ice Beam, TM15 Hyper Beam, TM24 Thunderbolt, TM26 Earthquake, TM29 Psychic, TM30 Shadow Ball, TM35
Flamethrower**); moves that are rarely TMs (Knock Off, Will-O-Wisp, Body Press, Psychic Fangs, Throat Chop), Cormoria's
themes (Ghost for Hollow Hill and the Necropolis, Ice for Mirroh), and **Bitter Blade** for any Pokémon with a sword or claw.

Shape: TM01-12 are the early ones (cheap coverage, mostly with a Speed or stat drop that helps catching and Sunder); the
rest are mid and late game. Where each TM is found or sold is decided area by area. TMs stay reusable (I_REUSABLE_TMS).
41 physical, 30 special, 19 status; every type has at least two attacks.

## Who can learn what

Compatibility comes from each Pokémon's canonical TM and tutor lists across the generations (the expansion's
`tools/learnset_helpers/teachable.py` builds them from `porymoves_files`), so most of these need no hand work.
**Bitter Blade** is canonically Ceruledge's alone; the rule asked for is *any Pokémon with a sword or claw*: proposed
as every Pokémon whose canonical moves include a blade or claw move (Slash, Night Slash, Leaf Blade, Sacred Sword, Psycho
Cut, Shadow Claw, Dragon Claw, Metal Claw, Crush Claw, Hone Claws), added through `porymoves_files/custom.json`, then
the list shown for a once-over.

**Sappy Seed** (user 2026-11-27) is canonically Partner Bulbasaur's only (Let's Go); the rule asked for is *most Grass
types*: every Grass-type species can learn it through `custom.json`, the list shown for a once-over (a few may be left out
where it would not fit). It is rare: **found around the Alban estate as a big prize** (user 2026-11-27), never sold.
