User 2026-11-27: map every wild Pokémon opportunity in Chapter 1. The player must be able to build a team that beats Kimaris (Trial 1, cap 18). Besides the starter, the shiny-tease Wimpod and the E. Rufflet, they need a lot of options. Every route gets at least one really rare encounter, about 1%, for players who like to hunt.

Pelluca has no grass, so the first wild grass is Route 11. The bible's "Pelluca grass tables" (§16.3 item 7) has nothing to stand on unless grass is added. The Necropolis has no wild Pokémon either. That fits the lore: the Langerins keep their grounds clear of the restless dead.

No rod and no Surf are given in Chapter 1 today. Pelluca's fishing table is Dreamstone Mysteries' (Arrokuda, Magikarp, Hisuian Qwilfish, Clauncher, all level 25).

## Proposed rules

1. **Land slot rates go back to Gen 3's: 20 / 20 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 5 / 5 / 4 / 4 / 1 / 1.** The rates today are Dreamstone Mysteries' 20 / 20 / 10 / 10, then 5 in every other slot, which leaves no slot rarer than 5%. The rates are one setting for the whole game, so this changes every map's table (the old DM maps are rebuilt later anyway). It gives every route **two 1% slots**:
   - a **Kimaris chase**, a rare that is a real answer to his team;
   - a **lore chase**, a rare that fits the place and is strong in its own right.
2. **Kept out of the wild, so choices keep their weight (Principle 2):**
   - the starters' lines (Frigibax, Teddiursa, Tinkatink);
   - the Burglar Egg's three (Squirtle, Chespin, Chimchar; user 2026-11-27);
   - Wimpod;
   - Rufflet and Braviary;
   - Eiscue (Loulan's);
   - Pancham (Rip's pride);
   - Spiritomb;
   - Bagon and Turtonator (Glaive's; Turtonator becomes catchable later).
   
   **No Dragon types:** in this era dragons are wild and hostile, and Glaive is the first to tame them.
3. **No spider anywhere in Chapter 1.** Finn has never seen whatever spins the red webs, and that should stay true. *(Open: if the spinner is meant to be found later, it stays out of every table until then.)*
4. **Levels follow the trainers.** Route 11's trainers are 9-10, Hollow Hill's 11-14, Rip 13-15, and Kimaris's cap is 18. After Trial 1 the cap rises to 25 for Trial 2.
