## 2. Route 11

**The farmhouse** (three people; the fog and the Marauders on their minds):
- **Farmer** (Farmer walker): Marauders took two of my Mareep off the hill pasture last moon, and the Templars came a day too late. The drovers say the gang answers to a red-handed rogue they call Rougehand, who'll sell anything that breathes. I keep my Pokémon in the house now.
- **His wife** (Commoner Woman A), before the theft: A Templar lass stopped here at dawn for water, with a little bird in her arms the like of which I've never seen. Black as pitch on one side, white as milk on the other. She was bound for Pelluca and in a hurry.
  After the theft: We bar the shutters when the fog comes down off Hollow Hill. Some nights you can hear marching in it, and nobody lives out there to march.
- **Their daughter** (Kid A): Papa says the Templars will catch the Marauders. Mama says the Templars have greater worries, up on the mountain.

**The west beach trainer: Fisher Tam** (Krabby Lv.9, Remoraid Lv.10)
- Before: The tide's out and the fish won't bite, so you'll have to do.
- Defeated: Bested by a man in a mask. My wife will never believe it.
- After: Fishing's poor along this coast of late. Something big moves past the cove at night, and every fish with sense has fled it.

**The theft** (the track at Route 11's west end; Loulan and her shiny Eiscue facing the Goon, the Bandit holding the chick):
- **Loulan:** Give it back! That chick is in the Archbishop's own keeping!
- Goon: Then the Archbishop ought to keep it better. Rip will pay well for a bird like this.
- Bandit: A Templar and now a Rainhawk? Time we were gone. *(they run into Hollow Hill)*
- **Loulan:** Rainhawk, thank Arceus. Those were Marauders, and they've taken a Rufflet chick unlike any other living, one I was raising for Archbishop Ambrose. ¶ I took a blow to the arm and can't give chase. Please, bring it back to me. I'll be waiting for you at Templar HQ, north of Mirroh.

## 3. Hollow Hill

*Rip speaks broad cockney, heavy on over-the-top English slang and comic nonsense, and calls himself "ol' Rip" (user 2026-11-26).*

**The paranoid officer: Royal Officer Denthorn** (class "Royal Officer"; Growlithe Lv.10, Machop Lv.10)
- Before: Halt! A mask, in these woods? You're one of Rip's, I'd stake my pay on it.
- Defeated: Whatever you are, you're no common cutthroat.
- Talked to after: I owe you an apology, Rainhawk. Marauders have robbed every cart through these woods, and half of them hide their faces. I see a mask and I see a thief.

**The far side: Rip takes the chick**
- **Rip:** Cor, lookit this! A black-an'-white Rufflet, an' not another like it in all Cormoria, so 'elp me. The Beads'd cough up a king's ransom for this little beauty, or some posh lord wiv more coin than sense. Ol' Rip's gonna be rollin' in it, innit!
- Goon: Boss! The masked one followed us.
- **Rip:** Oi oi! Show the Rainhawk 'ow Marauders say 'ello, you two!
- **Double battle: Goon (Poochyena Lv.10, Zubat Lv.10) and Bandit (Purrloin Lv.10, Murkrow Lv.11).** Goon defeated: Should've stayed a cooper. Bandit defeated: Rip's going to skin us.
- **Rip:** Gormless, the pair o' yer! Couldn't fight yer way out of a wet sack. Stand aside an' let ol' Rip sort out this bleedin' mess.
- **Rip 1v1: Stunky Lv.12, Sneasel Lv.12, Pawniard Lv.14** (Pawniard as his ace, growing with each meeting: Bisharp, then Kingambit). Defeated: Strike me pink! You beat the unholy piss outta me!
- **Rip:** Right, keep the bloomin' bird, guv'nor. Ol' Rip knows when 'e's been 'ad, but I've got more schemes than a mangy Poochyena's got fleas. Toodle-pip, Rainhawk, you ain't seen the last o' Rip! *(the three run off north)*
- The Rufflet cries, hops to the player (a "!" and a heart emote), and joins: "{PLAYER} received the Rufflet!" then the nickname prompt.

**Templar HQ, the quest's end** (with the Citadel scene, stage 4)
- **Loulan:** You brought it back! And look at it, perched on your arm as if it were born there. ¶ The Archbishop and I are agreed: it has chosen you, and it would only pine in our cages. Keep it, and take one of these for your trouble. *(choice: Metronome or Quick Claw)*
