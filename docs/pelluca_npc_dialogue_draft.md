# Pelluca NPC dialogue, draft v2 (2026-11-26, for the user's approval)

Rules: bible §16.15. Every line carries a hint or real world-building, spoken in a medieval manner, three or four
sentences at most. *(flavour)* = a small detail the bible does not state, sitting on canon.

## Pelluca City
- **Townswoman** (bridge): Masons out of Rivetshore raised this bridge, House Stone's men every one. Their guild has raised the toll on the coast road again, third time in two years. Where all that coin goes, none can say. *(flavour: the toll)*
- **Adventurer:** I meant to cross the Hoya Delta and see the Ashland Labyrinth, but the Templars bar the east road and won't say why. Just as well, says my father. He's not trusted an Ashland since the Prince-Consort was murdered.
- **Minstrel:** Worry is for tax collectors, friend. "Three stones had Cormor, and Keerin at his side..." There's a second verse, but it fell out of the song long ago, and no one in all Cormoria can give it back to me.
- **Townswoman:** A new face! Have a care on the east road. There's a bend in the Hoya by the old altar where no boatman will tie up after dark, and none will say why.
- **Townsman** (looking north): Templar riders, flying west for Mirroh. Third pair since morning. My cousin writes that the whole mountain groaned one night two winters past, and a strange light shone from the caves till dawn. *(flavour: the groan, the light)*
- **Farmer** (Stantler): Easy, girl, leave the Academy's flowers be. Her dam came from the Vilethorn country, where two of the Albans' field hands walked into the woods last harvest and never walked out. The family raised no search. *(flavour: the field hands)*
- **Stantler:** The Stantler is calmly munching the flowers.

## Harbour
- **Old woman** (shrine keeper): unchanged.
- **Harbourman:** My cousin has sat two winters in Gleamdusk for a brace of trout, waiting on the Concordance to judge him. Half those lords haven't sat their seats in a year, folk say. A poor man may rot while they're about their own affairs.
- **Woodsman** (Lycanroc): Bound for Mirroh? Don't stop in the fog at Hollow Hill for anything you hear. My Lycanroc won't pass the treeline, and there's red webbing in those trees thick as ship's rigging. Thirty years in the woods, and I've never seen the spider that could spin it.
- **Child:** Whee! I'm the Champion and you're the wicked dragon! Rhydia's beaten everyone for five whole years. When I'm grown I'll beat her, and she'll have to give me Justice!
- **Townsman** (tarts): Six apple tarts, and all of them Moray apples. They say Vizier Moray rides out to the Empress-mother's villa near every day, bearing the first of the harvest himself. Ten years of it. There's a loyal man for you. *(flavour: the apples)*

## Tavern
- **Innkeeper:** Welcome in, traveller! A hot meal, a cool cup, and your Pokémon set to rights. Shall I bring something out for them? / yes: Right away. ... Fed, watered and rested. The Templars keep this hearth for any trainer on the road. / no: As you like. The kitchen fire never goes out.
- **Barkeep:** Provisions for the road? Have a look. / after: Safe roads. Aye, the prices rose again. Every ball and tonic on that shelf comes through Sylphon & Stone, and their factor wants his coin before the crates are opened.
- **Mariner:** Two ships in this week, and one came round by Sevii. Kanto's bird-priests have taken the old chapel of Arceus for their own, her crew say. And still the skirmishes off Johto, for all the talk of peace. But you'd know more of that than I, Rainhawk. *(flavour: the chapel)*
- **Fisherman:** Something's breeding in the water by the sealed cave. My nets come up in ribbons, and every time there's Crawdaunt in them. More than I've ever seen in one place.
- **Sage:** (first time only) That mask is Athanto work. I'd not thought to see another, now the kingdom has fallen. Two years gone, and still none can say rightly how. ¶ (every time) A new name, a forgotten art, a change of temper... What would you have of me? / Come again whenever you've need.
- **Caretaker:** I tend the Pokémon a traveller can't carry on the road. Shall I fetch yours? / after: They're in good hands. Half the beasts I board belong to sailors who won't sail east, with Paldea's lords at each other's throats and Kalos little better.

## Dormitory
- **Cook:** Supper's at sundown. We had a Tellian lad in this kitchen till word came his tribes were taking up arms against each other. He wrote once, and never again.
- **Student A** (tables): On debate nights a body can't hear himself think in here. Last week we argued whether Cormor Dream-Eater ever lived at all. Master Orphel never once said which side he favoured.
- **Student B** (stage): A second-year argued at the lectern that the Empress-mother ought to leave her villa and sit her own throne. Half the hall cheered. The other half went very quiet.
- **Student C** (upstairs): Quiet hours begin at dark, and of late I'm the one who forgets. I sat up reading of House Jalviss, who found Cormoria's first Mega Stones and died of them to the last. Their ruins are still out there, they say, and something still in them.

## Academy classroom (new)
- **Logan:** You'll be the Rainhawk the Headmaster spoke of. Logan Orphel. The Headmaster built this school so trainers would know their Pokémon before they learn to win with them. I ask questions, and I remember who answers them.
- **Student A:** Master Orphel calls on whoever looks least ready. I make a point of always looking ready.
- **Student B:** Master Orphel took top honours in the Headmaster's first class. He's the last of House Orphel, and all that old name rests on him alone.
- **Student C:** I still can't see why a Ghost shrugs off a Normal blow. They say Baron Kimaris coos at his Spiritomb like a babe in swaddling. Can that be true of a Reserve Champion?
