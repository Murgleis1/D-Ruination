**The thematic shape of the v0.9.7 reordered gauntlet:**
- **Fight 1 (Rock Vizier):** Establishes the elevated combat tier — a senior Vizier fight with no special narrative loading; the player's signal that they have crossed from Trial-tier into Vizier-tier
- **Fight 2 (Umbra):** Faces Osrid's longest-trusted ally, who carries the third starter — the *first* Vizier with deep prior relationship to Osrid is fought to claim institutional eligibility for the throne. *Umbra is testing his pupil one final time.*

- **Fight 4 (Glaive):** Partnership is reaffirmed. Glaive's heart fully opens. The Vizier of War turns toward peace. *The partnership-thesis layer is resolved.*

**Sequence resolves three thematic layers (corruption / partnership / family) in succession:** Silas's defeat purges corruption → Glaive's defeat reaffirms partnership → Rhydia's Champion battle (next major beat) reconciles family. **The climactic ascent is a tightly-structured emotional progression**, each beat clearing one of the project's central thematic concerns before the next can land.

**The Sealing Cataclysm `[LOCKED — UPDATED v0.9.4]`:**

- **Silas Moray uses his psychic powers** to constrain Baradus and destroy the resonance bond Chien-Pao is forcing on him
- The combination of: destroying the resonance bond + the cosmic energy released by sealing all four Treasures simultaneously = **psychic energy beyond what humans can channel**
- **Silas dies from the psychic backlash.** His final act — what he himself would call thaumaturgy — is the only uncoerced act of love he ever performed. **Pierra is saved by Silas's death** (the cult threat that explicitly aimed at killing her is ended). The role he played in delusion for two decades is finally played for real.
- **Osrid survives the cataclysm with his memory intact.** Earlier drafts framed the sealing as causing amnesia; this is REVISED — **the sealing leaves Osrid physically alive, mentally intact, but presumed dead by Cormoria.** The cataclysm's chaos provides cover for his disappearance.
- **All four Treasures are sealed.** The Distortion gate is *not* hijacked; Phancero's potential re-emergence pathway is closed. This is the canon-consistent outcome — the Paldean king inherits and reinforces this seal ~2000 years later in SV.
- **Cormor does not need to wake.** The cosmic crisis has been resolved by mortal effort (Section 9 — Cormor's True Status). The greatest hero of all time continues to rest because his descendant has proven worthy.

**The thematic resolution:**
- Osrid sees what was possible (Nemo+Ting-Lu's coexistence). He cannot extend it. The world isn't ready.
- He settles for sealing — knowing future generations might reach the ideal. **The world that DM occupies (the post-modern Pokemon canon) is the slow realization of Osrid's lost vision, achieved across two millennia without Treasures.**
- **Osrid survives. He chooses to disappear.** The cataclysm gives him cover; Cormoria mourns him as dead; he uses the mistaken-death-narrative to slip away from the prince-identity and the empire he saved.

**Epilogue — Veddev (the renunciation, not the amnesia) `[LOCKED — UPDATED v0.9.4]`**

This is the project's most distinctive narrative move and one of its boldest departures from genre conventions. **Osrid remembers everything but chooses to discard his old identity completely and move forward into a new life with Eden.**

- Vandras Amber-Letz finds Osrid post-cataclysm — **Osrid's starter is with him** (per the unboxable-starter mechanic; Section 6) — out of its Pokeball
- **The starter runs up to Vandras, who recognizes the starter and then Osrid's face**
- Vandras almost speaks the recognition — *"Wait...is this..."* — **but does not finish the thought.** *He chose compassion-over-recognition* (Section 7 — Vandras Amber-Letz entry; the substantially revised v0.9.4 framing)
- Vandras gives him the name **Veddev** — a name with no political weight, no royal blessing. *A gift of new identity from a man who recognized the lost prince and chose to let him become someone new.*
- **Osrid remembers everything.** His memory was not wiped by the cataclysm. He retains every memory of his childhood, his exile, the Trials, the unmasking, the Champion battle, the cult crisis, Silas's death, Nemo's death, the sealing.
- **Osrid chooses to accept Veddev as his name going forward.** He chooses not to return to Cormoria. He chooses not to inform Pierra or Rhydia that he survived.

**Why Osrid chooses Veddev:**
- He set out to save his family and right the wrongs done to his father. **He did that.** The "revenge" — in the sense of paying back what was owed — is complete.
- He has no desire to be a prince or rule Cormoria. **The role is finished.**
- He believes that a brand new name, a new family (Vandras's), and (eventually) Eden returning into his life are all he needs to move forward into the future.
- **He is leaving the past behind for good, but keeping what is ultimately most important to him for the future.**

**The journey to Paldea:**
- Veddev travels east with Vandras's entourage
- Sells the four sealed cursed objects to the treasure-loving Paldean king — the canonical Paldean sealer per SV lore. **Osrid's role in canonical history is preserved** (he handed the Treasures to the king who would seal them; the canonical SV setup remains intact).
- **The Amber-Letz name passes through Veddev's line, evolving to Berlitz across ~1,800 years** — eventually producing Volo and Cynthia. **The line that produces Cynthia descends from a man who knowingly chose to let a fallen prince become someone new** — a *genealogy of deliberate compassion*.

**Eden recognizes Veddev during the transaction `[LOCKED]`:**
- Eden walks into the transaction. Veddev sees her. **His committed-to-forgetting collapses at exactly the place where it could not hold.**
- Not love-at-first-sight in the conventional sense. **It is conscious memory refusing to be renounced when faced with its actual referent.** Veddev sees Eden and the renunciation breaks open. *The presence of Eden completes his renunciation by giving him something better to choose than the past.*
- Eden recognizes him in stages: **body language first** (pre-verbal — she has loved this body's bearing for years), **then face**, then accepts the Veddev identity.
- **Eden uses "Veddev" going forward.** She does not insist on the prince-name. Her love adapts to who he has chosen to become.
- They marry in Paldea. **Veddev never returns to Cormoria.** Pierra and Rhydia never learn he survived.
- Implied: they may seal the Treasures further and establish the proto-detective tradition
- **In Cormoria, meanwhile:** Pierra survives. Rhydia continues as Champion. The empire enters a partially-collapsed but enduring restoration period. Pierra mourns Osrid as dead from the cataclysm — *she never learns he survived.* She lives on with Rhydia. **The classical tragedy of Pierra's arc** (Section 7) reaches its fullest weight: she loses Eldred, Silas, and Osrid (twice — to the false rumors and then to the renunciation she will never know about).
- **Vandras Amber-Letz never reveals what he knew.** He recognized Osrid in the moment he gave him the new identity, and he carries that secret across the rest of his life. **The greatest fan-project Pokemon family lineage we establish (Berlitz → Volo → Cynthia) descends from a man whose deliberate compassion gave a fallen prince peace.** Vandras's death takes the secret with him.
